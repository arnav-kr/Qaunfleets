# Demonstration

1. Open the recorded hybrid plan. Identify required energy, confirmed departures and the feeder limit.
2. Play the schedule. Compare charging visits with the tariff and site-demand charts.
3. Inspect the QCloud and Opt execution records and independent validation. Identify whether a result is recorded or freshly executed through the local SDKs.
4. Apply an early-departure or higher-building-load event at a slot boundary. Past allocations and delivered energy remain fixed; only the remaining horizon is replanned. Inspect actual departure shortfall separately from physical plan validation.
5. Refer to the saved evidence and [preliminary research](../research/README.md) for classical comparisons. State simulator, menu-size and prediction-domain limitations.

Displayed amounts are incremental EV charges in synthetic USD units. Vehicle movement is illustrative; it is not a traffic or routing model.
